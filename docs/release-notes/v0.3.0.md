## Firebase accounts and sync

- Sign in with Google or email and password.
- Keep using Hora offline; local tracking remains available without internet.
- Sync your activities, sessions, planned blocks, and tasks across devices when you reconnect.
- See whether your account is syncing, waiting for a connection, or safely stored locally.
- Keep each account's data separate on shared or previously used devices.

This release also includes Android build and update reliability fixes carried forward from the 0.2.0 release.
